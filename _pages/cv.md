## Profile

Earth system scientist and hydrologist combining computational modeling, environmental data science, and water-resources consulting. Experienced in developing and calibrating land-surface models, running large high-performance computing ensembles, applying machine learning, and translating geophysical and hydrological investigations into engineering decisions.

## Research experience

**Graduate Research Assistant**, College of Forestry, Wildlife and Environment, Auburn University — 2022–present

- Improved point-scale CLM5 simulations at three NEON flux towers, reducing GPP overestimation by 30% and ET underestimation by 10%.
- Reconstructed Mobile–Tensaw Delta salinity dynamics with machine learning to investigate environmental thresholds, vegetation responses, and coastal wetland vulnerability.
- Lead the Community Land Model contribution to CalLMIP, coordinating configuration and analysis for a study spanning 21,000 site–parameter combinations at 31 flux-tower sites and more than 12 land-surface models.
- Developed reproducible workflows for environmental data processing, model simulations, and performance evaluation.
- Created seven open environmental data-science tutorials and interactive tools, also used in Auburn courses and research proposal development.

## Professional experience

**Hydrogeologist, Hydrologist, and Projects Manager**, Georama Engineering Services Ltd., Nairobi, Kenya — 2016–2017; 2019–2021

- Managed field investigations, subsurface interpretation, and technical reporting for water-resource and environmental feasibility decisions.
- Conducted geophysical surveys for more than 100 borehole projects in Kenya and Tanzania. Groundwater was encountered in over 90% of drilled boreholes; also supervised drilling, development, and pumping tests for five boreholes.
- Developed rainfall–runoff models for ungauged catchments to assess runoff, water availability, storage needs, and water-supply reliability.
- Used 2D geophysical imaging to assess three proposed dam sites and three oil-spill sites, guiding preliminary dam-axis, geotechnical sampling, soil sampling, and monitoring recommendations.
