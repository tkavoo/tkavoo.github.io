I am a Ph.D. candidate in Earth System Science and a Graduate Research Assistant at Auburn University. My research brings together land-surface modeling, observations, and data science to improve predictions of terrestrial water and carbon cycles.

I study how forests exchange water and carbon with the atmosphere, how model uncertainty affects those predictions, and how environmental change is reshaping coastal wetlands. I also develop environmental data-science tutorials and tools for research and teaching.
